06 / Production Roadmap

From First Idea
to Final Master.

A five-stage collaborative methodology structured to preserve spontaneous artist intuition while maintaining uncompromising commercial audio quality.

01
Stage 01

Discovery

We explore the artist's references, emotional goals, genre direction, tempo, and vocal timbre targets before touching a fader.

Phase: Alignment
02
Stage 02

Pre-Production

Song architecture mapping, key selection, arrangement drafting, click-track prep, and custom synthesizer sound design.

Phase: Composition
03
Stage 03

Recording

Focused live studio tracking. Lead vocals, harmony stacks, guitars, and percussion tracked through pure analog tube preamps.

Phase: Tracking
04
Stage 04

Mix & Master

SSL summing, 3D spatial pan width, analog tape saturation, frequency carving, and competitive loudness optimization.

Phase: Post-Polish
05
Stage 05

Delivery

Delivery of 24-bit 96kHz lossless masters, instrumental cuts, TV sync edits, acapellas, and full uncompressed stem archives.

Phase: Delivery
